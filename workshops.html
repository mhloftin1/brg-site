What We Cover

AI for Scripting & Automation

How to use AI tools for the repetitive writing, communication, and documentation tasks that eat your day. Not theory. You'll build something in the session.

Multi-AI Workflows

How to hand work between Claude, ChatGPT, and other tools to get better output than any single tool produces alone. The briefing-a-colleague model.

Data Handling & Cleanup

Turn messy spreadsheets, inconsistent records, and scattered files into usable, structured data. The unsexy work that makes everything else possible.

Deep Research Techniques

How to use AI to answer real business questions (market research, competitive analysis, regulatory questions) faster and more thoroughly than traditional methods.

Writing That Doesn't Sound Like AI

How to use AI as a drafting partner while maintaining a voice that sounds like a human wrote it. For proposals, client communication, marketing, and internal docs.
