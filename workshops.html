Workshop 1: How to AI

Most people using AI are getting generic output. The problem isn't the tool — it's that the tool doesn't know anything about them.

This session fixes that. In three hours you'll learn what a brand document is, why it's the foundation for everything else, and how to onboard AI into your business as a tool that actually knows your operation — not just a toy you experiment with.

We run a single example throughout: a solo insurance agent named Sandy. Every concept gets applied through three lenses — how you communicate, how you work, and what clients actually experience.

What you'll leave with
  • A clear understanding of what a brand document is and why it's the foundation for everything else
  • A working mental model for how to onboard AI into your business — not as a toy, but as a tool that knows your operation
  • The prompt structure and materials you need to build your brand doc before Workshop 2
  • Three Monday actions that take 30 minutes and set up everything that comes next
